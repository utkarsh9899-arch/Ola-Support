# Service Credit Policy

Service credits are non-cash Ola Money credits given to riders to make up for a service failure that does not qualify for a refund. Typical triggers are a driver arriving more than 15 minutes late, a missed SLA on the rider's own ticket, or a documented poor-quality ride. Credits range from INR 50 to INR 300 per incident, and a single rider can receive at most INR 1,000 in service credits per calendar month. Service credits expire 90 days after they are issued and cannot be transferred or withdrawn as cash.
