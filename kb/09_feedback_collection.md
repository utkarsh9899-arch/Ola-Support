# Feedback Collection Process

When a ticket is marked Resolved, the customer receives an in-app survey asking for a satisfaction score from 1 to 5 and an optional comment. The survey stays open for 7 days, and a ticket with no survey response is automatically moved to Closed when that window ends. Any score of 1 or 2 opens a review task for a team lead, who must contact the customer within 48 hours. Survey results are combined every week into a customer satisfaction (CSAT) report broken down by city and ticket category.
