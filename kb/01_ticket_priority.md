# Ticket Priority Classification Rules

Every Ola support ticket is assigned one of four priority levels when it is created. P1 (Critical) covers rider or driver safety incidents and any issue that blocks a trip already in progress. P2 (High) covers payment failures, double charges, and account lockouts that stop a rider from booking or a driver from going online. P3 (Medium) covers fare disputes, app bugs that have a workaround, and product defects that do not block service. P4 (Low) covers general inquiries, feature requests, and feedback that needs no corrective action.
