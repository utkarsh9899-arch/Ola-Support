# VIP Customer Handling Policy

Riders in the Elite loyalty tier and employees of corporate key accounts are treated as VIP customers. VIP tickets are routed to a dedicated priority desk and receive a first response within half of the standard SLA time for their priority level. Each VIP ticket is assigned a single named owner who stays responsible for it until the ticket is closed. VIP status does not change refund eligibility, but the priority desk may raise the service-credit limit for a VIP customer to INR 500 per incident.
