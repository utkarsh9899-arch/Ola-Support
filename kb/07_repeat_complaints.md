# Repeat Complaint Handling Policy

A complaint counts as a repeat complaint when the same customer raises the same category of issue three or more times within 30 days. Repeat complaints are automatically raised by one priority level and routed to an L2 senior specialist instead of the general queue. The specialist must review every previous related ticket, identify the root cause, and confirm a permanent fix with the customer. If a single driver is the subject of three repeat complaints within 30 days, that driver's account is flagged for a quality review by the city operations team.
